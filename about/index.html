Why deliberately small beats big.

Most IT providers measure success in ticket volume and billable hours. Their incentive is for things to break often enough to keep billing. Ours is the opposite: a short client list means problems get prevented, not milked.

You'll never be handed to a junior learning on your time. There are no juniors. The person who scopes your project is the person who builds it, and the person who answers when something goes wrong at 7am.

Being small also means we can go deeper than support. Because we hold the keys to your environment and understand how your business runs, we can build the custom software and automation most providers can only talk about.

A deliberately short client list, senior attention every time, and technology that quietly compounds as an advantage — that's the trade we offer.
