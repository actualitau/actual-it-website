FAQ
What's the difference between Foundations and Growth?

Foundations is three jobs a month on the site and Google listing you already have, plus the monthly report and an AI Answer Check. Growth includes all of that, and adds two new suburb or service pages a quarter.

FAQ
Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No. We don't promise rankings, traffic or enquiries. Search results and AI answers change. What we do is steady, practical work on your site, your listing and how assistants talk about you, then we show you what changed.

FAQ
What is an AI Answer Check?

We ask 10 questions a local customer would actually ask, of Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT (where it answers without a login). You get a baseline when we start and a re-check every month, with a plain record of what each assistant said. Any fixes come out of your monthly jobs.

FAQ
What counts as a job on the plan?

A job is one of: titles and metas on existing pages, one technical fix such as schema, a tidy of one existing page, a Google listing pass, or a directory citations pass. New pages, redesigns, photos, forms and body rewrites sit outside Foundations, unless they're the new pages already included in Growth. Contact us for a quote on that extra work.

FAQ
Can I get a one-off audit instead of a monthly plan?

Yes. The AI visibility audit is a one-off look at whether AI assistants recommend you for the local questions your customers ask. Contact us for pricing. If you later move onto a monthly plan, that audit becomes your baseline.
