FAQ
What's the difference between the custom site and WordPress?

The custom site has no editing back end. It's simpler to build, and we host it. WordPress gives you a self-edit back end so you can change pages and posts yourself. Contact us for pricing on either.

FAQ
Can I edit the custom-built site myself?

Not through a normal back end, because there isn't one. When something needs changing, we make the change, or we can set you up to do it yourself through Claude.

FAQ
What is the Claude setup?

It's a way for you to request or make site changes using Claude, instead of logging into a WordPress-style dashboard. We'll walk you through it if it's a good fit. If you'd rather not use it, just send us the change and we'll do it.

FAQ
What do you need from me before you start?

Three things: design inspiration (sites or styles you like, and ones you don't), a site structure (the pages you need and how they hang together), and the content and copy for those pages. We can't invent your business for you, and the site is better when the words are yours.

FAQ
Can you also help people find the new site?

Yes. Building the site and helping it get found are separate pieces of work. If you want ongoing local SEO and AI-answer checks once the site is live, that's our SEO service.
