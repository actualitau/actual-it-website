Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

How do you scope a project?

We map how your business actually operates first (systems, workflows, pain points), then design the technology around it. Fixed scope, fixed price, no surprises.

FAQ
We're moving office. Can you handle the whole IT move?

Yes. That's a core consultancy job: planning the network, coordinating providers, the move itself, and making sure everything works before your team arrives.

FAQ
Our tools don't talk to each other. Can you integrate them?

Usually, yes. Most business tools have APIs or import/export paths; we connect them so you stop re-typing data between systems.

FAQ
How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A network upgrade might be a week, a system migration two to six weeks. You get a clear timeline before we start.
