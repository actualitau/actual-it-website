Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

What is an AI agent, in plain terms?

An AI agent is software we set up to carry out a defined job, such as triaging enquiries or drafting a quote, using the tools you already have. It prepares the work. You review it, and you stay accountable for what goes out.

FAQ
Will it send email or act on its own?

Not unless you have agreed that it can, and even then the important actions ask for a person's approval. We start with drafts and recommendations, not a free hand.

FAQ
Is this Microsoft 365 Copilot?

No. Copilot sits inside Microsoft's apps and helps when you ask. An agent is set up to carry a specific job forward, often across mail, files and Teams, and we stay on it as a service. The two can work alongside each other.

FAQ
How much does it cost?

Get in touch for a quote. We'll look at the job with you and say what it would take before anything is built.

FAQ
Do we have to wait for a new platform?

No. We can start with a discovery now and build with the tools that are ready. If a supplier platform such as Pax8's 8bot becomes the better option later, we'll talk that through with you before changing anything.

FAQ
What do you need from us to start?

A clear job that's eating time every week, access to the relevant Microsoft 365 mail, files or Teams (you approve the connections), and someone who can review the agent's work in the first few weeks.

FAQ
Will this replace staff?

No. The point is to take repetitive work off a small team, not to replace the people who make the decisions. Judgement and accountability stay with you.

FAQ
Where does our data go?

We work inside your Microsoft 365 tenancy wherever that's the right place, and we limit what the agent can see to the job at hand. If any work would run on a supplier platform, including one hosted outside Australia, we'll tell you before it's switched on.
